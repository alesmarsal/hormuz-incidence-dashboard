  • Arab Light price: OPEC, 33 issues, latest: OPEC Monthly Oil Market Report – November 2026 (released 2024-03-12 to 2026-11-11); OPEC Monthly Oil Market Report, table 'Table 1 - 1: OPEC Reference Basket and selected crudes, US$/b', row 'Arab Light'.
  • Saudi crude exports and production (published months): JODI (Joint Organisations Data Initiative), JODI-Oil World Database, world primary table (released 2026-10-21); JODI-Oil World Database, primary table: REF_AREA=SA, ENERGY_PRODUCT=CRUDEOIL, FLOW_BREAKDOWN=TOTEXPSB, UNIT_MEASURE=KBD.
  • Saudi crude production, OPEC secondary sources (estimated months): OPEC, 32 issues, latest: OPEC Monthly Oil Market Report – November 2026 (released 2024-04-11 to 2026-11-11); OPEC Monthly Oil Market Report, table 'OPEC (from May 2024: DoC) crude oil production based on secondary sources, tb/d', row 'Saudi Arabia'.
  • Saudi supply to the market, direct communication (range): OPEC, 9 issues, latest: OPEC Monthly Oil Market Report – November 2026 (released 2025-09-11 to 2026-11-11); OPEC Monthly Oil Market Report, table 'OPEC crude oil production based on direct communication, tb/d', footnote to row 'Saudi Arabia'.
  • OPEC+ required production in force on 28 February 2026 (pre-war volume): OPEC, 5 issues, latest: OPEC press release: Eight OPEC+ countries' meeting of 1 March 2026: unwinding resumed, +206 kb/d in April 2026 (released 2025-11-02 to 2026-03-01); Required production table, page 2 of the eight-country press releases (image).
  • Brent forecast of February 2026 (pre-war price): U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA), Short-Term Energy Outlook, February 2026 (released 2026-02-10); Table 2, PDF page 36, row 'Brent Spot Average'.
  • Brent spot price (Arab Light differential): U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA), Europe Brent Spot Price FOB (Dollars per Barrel), Daily (released 2026-11-12); EIA workbook RBRTEd.xls, sheet 'Data 1'.
  • Nominal GDP 2025 (per cent of GDP): General Authority for Statistics (GASTAT), GDP and National Accounts Q2/2026 (released 2026-09-08); table 1.1: gross domestic product by main economic activities at current prices (million ).
